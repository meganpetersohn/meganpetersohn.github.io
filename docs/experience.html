Field experience

Ten years of carnivore and ungulate fieldwork across North America

Field projects

Yellowstone Wolf, Cougar, and Elk Project · National Park Service, Yellowstone National Park, Wyoming · Nov 2022 to Apr 2023
GPS-cluster investigations, behavioural observation, and camera monitoring for a 28-year continuous winter predator-prey study, in one of North America’s most intensively studied wolf systems.
Wolf and Deer Project · U.S. Geological Survey, Ely, Minnesota · Jul 2020 to Aug 2022
Managed the final phase of one of the world’s longest-running wolf-deer research programs as the sole field technician: aerial telemetry, live-capture, necropsies, winter tracking, and long-term data management in the Superior National Forest and Boundary Waters region. This is where my wolf pup survival project began.
Isle Royale Wolf Introduction · SUNY College of Environmental Science and Forestry, Isle Royale National Park, Michigan · May to Aug 2019
More than 100 GPS-cluster investigations during the first year of wolf reintroduction to a roadless island, documenting early predation patterns, prey selection, and social dynamics among reintroduced wolves. I returned with the National Park Service in March 2020.
Mexican Wolf Recovery Program · U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Alpine, Arizona · Oct 2018 to Apr 2019 and Jan to May 2020
Live-capture and monitoring of endangered Mexican wolves, with conflict mitigation (hazing, deterrents, fladry, fencing) across agency and ranching contexts in the Gila National Forest, and fieldwork in partnership with the White Mountain Apache Tribe and San Carlos Apache Tribe.

New Mexico Department of Game and Fish · Mount Taylor, New Mexico · May to Sep 2018

Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources · Dodgeville, Wisconsin · Nov 2017 to May 2018

Mississippi State University · Baraga, Michigan · Apr to Nov 2017

Skills from the field

Multi-platform tracking (fixed-wing, helicopter, watercraft, snow travel, and extended backcountry travel), live-capture and chemical immobilization, telemetry, remote cameras, GPS-cluster investigations, necropsies, and biological sampling. See my CV for certifications.
