CV

Download CV (PDF)

I am a wildlife ecologist and M.Sc. candidate with extensive carnivore-ungulate field experience and a commitment to collaborative, community-engaged research. I am broadly interested in researching how large mammals move, interact, and coexist across working landscapes, integrating spatial ecology, predator-prey dynamics, and human-wildlife interactions to inform conservation and management.

Research expertise

  • Quantitative ecology and data management. R, GitHub version control, GIS (ArcGIS/Collector), spatial data processing, long-term dataset management, and transparent, reproducible analytical workflows.
  • Field-based predator-prey research. Multi-platform tracking (fixed-wing, helicopter, watercraft, snow travel, and extended backcountry travel), live-capture, and telemetry of carnivores and ungulates; remote camera deployment and servicing; GPS-cluster investigations, necropsies, biological sampling, and long-term wildlife research.
  • Community partnerships and engagement. Ongoing research collaboration, knowledge exchange, and youth engagement initiatives with Splatsin; prior fieldwork in partnership with the White Mountain Apache Tribe and San Carlos Apache Tribe; and conflict mitigation (hazing, deterrents, fladry, fencing) and public engagement across high-conflict working landscapes.
  • Teaching, mentorship, and scientific communication. Teaching assistant, undergraduate supervision, scientific writing, conference presentations, and inclusive instruction and mentorship.
